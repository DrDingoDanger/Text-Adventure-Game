Game Objective


* The goal of the game is to obtain an emerald
* To do this you should find items by exploring areas and killing mobs
* Found items can be traded with certain NPCs to obtain new items


Food/Hunger


* Moving between locations lowers your hunger
* Running out of hunger causes you to lose the game
* Using food items restores hunger, different foods restore different amounts of hunger


Health/Damage


* Losing all of your health will cause you to lose the game
* Encounters with mobs damage you and the mob, killing the mob will put its drop items directly into your inventory
* Using weapon items will modify the amount of damage you do


Other


* All inputs are through text commands, make sure to carefully read everything
* Remember to explore all locations and interact with NPCs
* In addition to NPCs that sell you stuff, others give you useful information
* There is 1 secret additional way to lose
